# Bashfx VIP GOLD ROOM — Revised Tutorial Production Plan

## Approved Production Definition

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Give traders a practical, step-by-step tour of the private Bashfx VIP GOLD ROOM journal and its expanded multi-market workflow. |
| Audience | Authenticated Forex, metal, index, and commodity traders using the journal for daily M5 trade review. |
| Duration | Approximately 4 minutes 15 seconds. |
| Format | 1080p landscape, 16:9. |
| Visual direction | Black-and-gold luxury trading-desk aesthetic. Use genuine app-screen captures for every product demonstration, framed by understated gold motion graphics and gold touch/click rings. |
| Narration | English, warm, smooth male voice: Algieba. Calm, deliberate instructional pace. |
| Captions and music | No subtitles and no background music. Light interface-like click accents only. |
| Accessibility | Large, high-contrast callouts; no information is conveyed by colour alone. |

## Narrative Arc

The walkthrough moves from sign-in and dashboard orientation to daily multi-pair trade capture, Trader Name, weekly review and report generation, monthly reporting, Community feedback, and private WAT reminders. It closes with an actionable daily workflow recap.

## Clip Requirements

Each demonstration scene retains the same black-and-gold interface treatment. A thin gold circle traces to the selected control, performs one subtle pulse, then fades before the next action. The camera uses slow, readable screen crops and gentle push-ins; there are no rapid cuts, no simulated data entry, and no fabricated feedback, ratings, or trade results.

| Clip group | Pacing | Screen focus | Transition treatment | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Moderate | Dashboard and sidebar | Gold line draws across the frame, resolves into the dashboard; the sidebar remains visible throughout the move. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
| Daily journal | Moderate | Date and new trade card | Camera pushes into the date field, then pans to the pair selector and session controls; gold ring follows the route without obscuring the field. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
| Multi-pair selection | Moderate | Market-pair selector | The list opens from the selected control; the surrounding UI stays still while a gold focus ring moves between pair groups. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
| Trader Name | Slow | Trader Name field | A gold underline expands under the label, then a confirmation tick appears beside the saved field. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
| Weekly review | Moderate | Weekly log and Summary page | A vertical date grouping expands, then a scene cut carries the same gold focus ring to the Generate button. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
| Monthly reporting | Slow | Monthly Summary page | The schedule card stays visible as a gold clock hand moves to 01:05 WAT, then resolves to the finished report area. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
| Community and reminders | Moderate | Community and Weekly Summary reminder section | A scene cut changes from the feedback form to reminder controls; the WAT badge receives the final gold pulse. | Narration, soft click accents, no music. |
