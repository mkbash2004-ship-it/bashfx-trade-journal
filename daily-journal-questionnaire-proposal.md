# Bashfx VIP GOLD ROOM — Daily Trade Journal Questionnaire

## Purpose

This form is designed to replace the image/PDF-first workflow with a fast daily journal. It separates information that applies to the **entire trading day** from information that must be captured for **every individual trade**. A daily journal can therefore contain one trade, five trades, or more without limiting the trader.

The weekly summary will calculate totals from the individual trade cards, while the daily context fields will make the Friday or Saturday review more meaningful.

## Confirmed Trading Profile

| Journal setting | Confirmed value |
|---|---|
| **Instrument** | XAUUSD (Gold) only |
| **Sessions** | London and New York only |
| **Execution timeframe** | M5 (five minutes) |

These three settings will be fixed defaults in the journal so you never have to repeat them unnecessarily. Each trade card will still ask which of the two sessions you traded.

> **How it works:** Start one daily journal entry, complete the day-level check-in, then press **Add another trade** for every trade taken that day. Each trade card is saved separately and can be edited before the weekly summary is generated.

## Part A — Daily Market Context

Complete this section once per trading day.

| Question | Answer format | Why it is recorded |

## Weekly Summary Fields Generated Automatically

The Friday/Saturday Bashfx VIP GOLD ROOM summary image will use the saved trade cards to generate the following automatically. No manual calculation is needed.

| Weekly output | Calculation / source |
|---|---|
| **Trade table** | XAUUSD, session, direction, pips, and result from every saved trade card. |
| **Total trades** | Count of all trade cards saved during the week. |
| **Wins, losses, and break-even trades** | Count of final-result values. |
| **Win rate** | Wins divided by wins plus losses; break-even trades are shown separately. |
| **Total pips and net P&L** | Sum of signed pip and profit/loss inputs. |
| **Best session and setup** | Performance grouping for London versus New York and the saved setup tags. |
| **CPI / news impact** | Count and notes from the daily market-context section. |
| **Discipline score** | Based on plan adherence, risk-limit adherence, and execution-discipline responses. |
| **Weekly lesson and next focus** | Drawn from end-of-day reflections. |

## Proposed Daily Flow

1. At the end of the trading day, open **Daily Journal** and complete Part A in approximately one minute.
2. Complete one trade card for each trade taken, using **Add another trade** as many times as needed.
3. Complete Part C once, then save the daily journal.
4. On Friday evening or Saturday, open **Weekly Summary**, review the calculated totals, and generate the branded Bashfx VIP GOLD ROOM image.
